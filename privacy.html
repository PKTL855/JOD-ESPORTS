JOD × ESPORTS

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

JOD x ESPORTS is a community esports tournament website and app run by the JOD x ARMY organizers. Players use it to register a team, book scrim and practice-lobby slots, and vote on upcoming events. This page explains what we collect and how you can delete it.

Who to contact

Organizer email: vilaiyattupullusamy@gmail.com

What we collect

  • Google sign-in details: your name, email address and profile photo, used only to log you in and link your team to your account.
  • Team details you enter: team name, IGL name, IGL in-game ID, mobile number and team logo (a photo you upload or an icon you pick).
  • Activity: the slots your team books and the option you vote for.

We do not access your Instagram account. The "I followed" tick is something you confirm yourself. We do not show ads and we do not sell or rent your data.

How we use it

  • To run team registration, slot booking and voting.
  • To contact teams about their matches.

Who can see it

  • Everyone: team name and team logo appear on the public slot lists.
  • Only you and the organizer: mobile number, IGL name, IGL in-game ID and email address.
  • If you tap "Send to organizer on WhatsApp", your booking message is sent through WhatsApp, which has its own privacy policy.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore, Mumbai region). The website is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts. These providers process data under their own privacy policies.

How long we keep it

We keep your data until you delete it or the organizer removes your team.

Delete your team and account

In the app: sign in, open Register and tap Delete my team & account. This removes your team details, slot bookings, vote and sign-in account.

Without the app: email vilaiyattupullusamy@gmail.com with your team name and the Google email you used. The organizer will delete your data and reply to confirm.

Children

JOD x ESPORTS is meant for players aged 13 and older.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
